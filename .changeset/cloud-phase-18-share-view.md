**Share a design project as a link anyone can open.** `maude share publish` takes pictures of your canvases on your own machine and puts them behind a private link — no install, no account, no git for whoever you send it to. `maude share off` turns it back off.

The shared view serves pictures and nothing else: no scripts, no live project, and no way for it to reach the project's data. That is why it is safe to hand to someone outside the team. SVG is deliberately not published — an SVG can carry code, and nothing on this surface is ever allowed to run.

Sharing is off until you turn it on, and every page says how old the pictures are, whether or not they look stale. They do not update themselves — publish again after the design changes.
