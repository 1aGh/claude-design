**Maude Cloud accounts.** You can now create an account and sign in at the control plane: email and password today, "Continue with Google" the moment its credentials are configured — both land on the *same* account, so nobody signs in twice. What Maude Cloud stores (and what it never does) is stated inside the signup form itself, and the consent is only recorded if you actually ticked it. Signing out ends the session on the server, not just in your browser. A signed-in owner can mint a short-lived, project-scoped pass for attaching a project — an expiring artifact of your account, never a credential to paste around.
