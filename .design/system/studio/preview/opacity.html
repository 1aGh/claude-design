Opacity

A canonical alpha ladder. The plasma accent reduced to seven discrete alphas covers every tint, glow, scrim, and disabled state in studio. Reach outside this set only when you have a measured reason.

0.08soft scrim
0.12--accent-soft
chip bg
0.18hover glow
0.25--accent-glow
shadow tint
0.30--shadow-sm
0.40--shadow-md
scrim
0.50--shadow-lg
dialog scrim

Don't reach for opacity: 0.6 on a clickable element. Below 0.5, the surface stops reading as "live"; below 0.4, screen readers may still find it but humans miss it.
