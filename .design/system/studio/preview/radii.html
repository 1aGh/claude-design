Radii

Six steps from tag (3px) to hero card (16px), plus pill (full). studio leans tight — most chrome uses --radius-sm or --radius-md; the lg/xl are reserved for cards and hero stages.

xs3px · tags, input borders
sm5px · input, kbd, pill-button
md7px · button, dialog
lg11px · card, panel
xl16px · hero stage
pill999px · badges

Don't mix scales on the same surface. A card with --radius-lg outside and --radius-md inside is fine — the radii decrease with elevation. A card with --radius-sm outside and --radius-lg inside is wrong.
