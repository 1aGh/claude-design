Elevation

Six shadows — four ambient (sm → xl) and two glows (accent, amber). Affinity-deep on the ambient ladder; glows are reserved for signature moments (logo, hero card, run-indicator).

--shadow-sm0 1px 2px · subtle separator
--shadow-md0 4px 16px · card hover, toast
--shadow-lg0 16px 48px · dialog, drawer
--shadow-xl0 32px 96px · command palette
--shadow-glowaccent glow · button hover, mark
--shadow-glow-amberbrand glow · marketing only

Stack shadows when needed: var(--shadow-lg), var(--shadow-glow) on a hover state combines depth + accent halo. Just don't ship that combination at rest.
