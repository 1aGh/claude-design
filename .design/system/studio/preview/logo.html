Logo

The studio mark is a plasma square with an amber inset rotated 45° — chrome plus a wink. The wordmark sets studio in Geist 600 with a plasma slash separator and a mono subscript naming the host project.

Mark — alone

The mark stands without the wordmark in places too small for type (favicon, dev-server tab, the topnav of a 1024px viewport). Default mark is plasma + amber inset; the amber-only alt is reserved for marketing surfaces where the surrounding chrome is plasma.

Construction

Mark — anatomy

Plasma square, radius --radius-lg, glow shadow on dark surfaces. Amber square inset at 22%, rotated 45°, scaled 0.78. The inset is what makes it a studio mark and not a generic plasma square.

Wordmark — anatomy

studio/md-claude

Geist 600 at tracking-display (-0.022em). The / is mono 500 in plasma. The subscript is mono 400 in --fg-2. Together, the lockup says "studio runs against this project" without a logo file.

On surface

The lockup reads on dark chrome (the default) and on the amber spotlight background (marketing hero only).

No-go zone

Patterns a brand-critic will catch. The completeness-critic doesn't check these; this section exists so a future reader knows the rule.

no flat mark — the shadow-glow is part of the identity. no missing inset — without amber, it's a generic plasma square. no swap — plasma is the body, amber is the inset, never the inverse.

Assets live at assets/logos/wordmark.svg and assets/logos/mark.svg. The SVGs use the OKLCH literal values from colors_and_type.css rather than CSS vars so they render outside the design-system context (favicon, GitHub avatar, npm registry).

If you change --accent or --brand-amber, also re-export the SVGs. Drift between tokens and assets is how brand systems decay.
