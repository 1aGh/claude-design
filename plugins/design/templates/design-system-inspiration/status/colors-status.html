Status colors

Four status semantics — success, warn, error, info. Separate from the accent family because actions and outcomes need distinct signals: accent says "do this", status says "this happened".

--status-successsaved / synced / live
--status-warndeferred / due soon
--status-errorfailed / blocked
--status-infonote / heads-up
