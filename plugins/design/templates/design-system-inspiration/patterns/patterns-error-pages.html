Error pages

Four full-page errors. Each names what went wrong in plain language, then offers a concrete next step.

404

Can't find that canvas

It might have been renamed, archived, or never existed. Check the canvas index, or start a new one.

500

Something broke on our end

We've logged it. Try again in a moment — if it persists, check the status page.

offline

You're offline

Recent edits are saved locally — they'll sync when the connection comes back.

maintenance

Quick tune-up in progress

We're rolling out faster sync. Should take about 5 minutes — your data is safe.
