Elevation

Three levels of shadow — and one explicit zero. Shadow is reserved for things that float above the page (popovers, dropdowns, dialogs). Stationary cards use border + bg step, not shadow.

flat border + bg-1
--shadow-sm hover lift
--shadow-md popover, menu
--shadow-lg dialog, sheet

If a card sits on the page surface and doesn't move, no shadow. Shadows imply "floats above" — use them when that's literally true.
