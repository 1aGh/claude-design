Motion

Four durations × two eases — every animation in the system maps to one of these tokens. Hover over each card to play the canonical motion for that role.

Flip
--dur-flip · --ease-out Small, snappy. Press-down, hover lift.
Panel slide
--dur-panel · --ease-in-out Drawer / sidebar entry.
Route enter
--dur-route · --ease-out Page transition. Opacity + tiny scale.
Soft fade
--dur-soft · --ease-out Toast, tooltip, soft reveal.

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) short-circuits every duration to 1ms. Never use !important to override — the cascade carries.
