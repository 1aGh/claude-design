Keyboard primitives

The <kbd> element with project-specific styling. Use Mac symbols (⌘ ⇧ ⌥ ⌃) on macOS and spell out on other platforms — the visual style is identical. Combos chain individual <kbd>s with a "+" separator.

Press ⌘+K from anywhere in the app to open the command palette. Use J and K to navigate the results, ⏎ to run the selected action, or ⎋ to dismiss without choosing.
